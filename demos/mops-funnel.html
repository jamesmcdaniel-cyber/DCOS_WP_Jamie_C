GAP-01Pipeline SourceAE/Rep self-sourced pipeline represents less than 12% of total pipeline volume across 1,395 SAL opportunities. In FY26, 9 of 16 territory reps contributed zero self-sourced deals in at least one quarter, creating dangerous over-reliance on inbound marketing and SDR motion to fill the top of funnel.CriticalDefine AE sourcing targets per segment; hold weekly pipeline source reviews in QBRs; tie self-sourced quota to comp plan
GAP-02Deal Qualification265 opportunities ($52M ACV) are stale 90d+ with engagement below 50 and no meetings in 90 days. The top account, Skyridge Aerospace Corp, has been sitting in Stage 1 for 889 days with an engagement score of 10.0 — the deal was originally sourced at a trade show where a VP handed Marcus Royster a business card, and no substantive follow-up has occurred since the initial discovery call.CriticalMandatory management review cadence for any deal 90d+ with no activity; force close/loss decision within 30 days or remove from pipeline
GAP-03MQL ConversionLead→Opp conversion rate is 2.7% (65 of 2,430 FY26 MQLs resulted in a subsequent opportunity). MQL disqualification rate is 68%, meaning roughly 2 out of every 3 leads that marketing passes to sales are immediately rejected. Non-customer MQLs disqualify at 69% vs 59% for existing customer accounts, suggesting ICP targeting drift in outbound programs.HighReview and tighten MQL scoring criteria; introduce account-level ICP fit score gate; improve SDR follow-up SLA from 72h to 24h
GAP-04Win Rate TrendQualified win rate (Stage 2+ closed deals only) is stable and strong: 63.3% (FY24), 60.5% (FY25), 63.9% (FY26). Earlier reporting showed win rates of 30–37% because Stage 0 and Stage 1 losses were incorrectly included in the denominator — those deals never reached qualified pipeline status and therefore do not reflect true competitive win/loss performance.HighStandardize win rate reporting methodology across all dashboards to use Stage 2+ denominator only; conduct formal win/loss analysis for all FY25–26 qualified losses
GAP-05New Business MixFY26 New Business ACV ($33.5M) is 38.9% of total won ACV vs. 15.3% in FY24 — a dramatic and positive shift toward net-new logo acquisition. However, upsell still contributes 61.6% of total won ACV, and 7 of the top 10 FY26 revenue deals were expansions of existing accounts rather than new logos.HighSet explicit new logo ACV targets at 45% of total for FY27; create dedicated new business overlay comp structure
GAP-06Sales Cycle VelocityFY26 median cycle (Stage 2 → Close, qualified deals only): Strategic 264d (↑ from 249d in FY25), Large Enterprise 107d (↓ from 236d — notable improvement), Small Enterprise 89d, Velocity 61d, Small Business 105d. The Strategic segment cycle has lengthened for two consecutive years. Only deals with a recorded Stage 2 date are included; unqualified pipeline is excluded.MediumConduct root cause analysis on Strategic cycle extension; identify top 3 deals that dragged median up; benchmark against peer companies
GAP-07MQL Customer ExpansionOnly 205 of 2,430 FY26 MQLs (8.4%) came from existing customer accounts. The marketing team ran three customer-targeted webinar series in FY26 but these generated fewer than 40 trackable MQLs combined, suggesting that either attendance was poor, attendees are not being captured as MQLs, or the content did not drive sufficient intent signals to qualify.MediumBuild dedicated customer marketing playbook; route customer intent signals directly to CSM team; set FY27 customer MQL target at 15%
GAP-08UTM / Campaign AttributionCampaign attribution data is inconsistently applied — approximately 34% of SAL-stage opportunities have no Salesforce campaign source tag, and UTM parameter naming conventions are not enforced across paid, organic, and event channels. This makes it impossible to calculate true cost-per-opportunity or cost-per-pipeline-dollar for any individual marketing channel.LowImplement and publish UTM standards documentation; audit all existing campaign source values in SFDC; gate new campaign creation on attribution tag compliance
